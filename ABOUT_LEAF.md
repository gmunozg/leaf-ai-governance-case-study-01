# About LEAF

LEAF works at the intersection of law, AI governance and business operations.

The focus is practical: helping organizations create structure around how AI is introduced, reviewed and managed.

Areas of interest include:

- AI governance
- AI risk management
- Legal technology
- Responsible AI
- AI vendor risk
- Human oversight
- Governance operations
- AI adoption workflows

LEAF's work is designed around a simple principle:

> AI governance should make better decisions easier to make, explain and revisit.
