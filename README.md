# LEAF AI Governance Case Study 01

## AI Use-Case Governance & Approval System

A self-directed portfolio case study exploring how an organization can introduce structure around the adoption of AI systems.

The project focuses on a practical business question:

> How can a company decide whether a new AI use case should move forward, what needs to be reviewed, and how that decision can be documented?

## What this project demonstrates

- Translating AI governance principles into a usable business process
- Applying NIST AI RMF concepts to a simulated enterprise scenario
- Thinking across Legal, Privacy, Security and business risk
- Designing structured review and decision-making processes
- Building traceability and accountability into AI adoption
- Identifying where human judgment should remain central

## Scenario

The simulated organization is considering an AI system that supports commercial contract review.

The case study examines how an organization could approach questions around risk, oversight, evidence, controls and governance before allowing real-world use.

## Framework

This portfolio project is informed by the NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0.

The project does not claim NIST certification or NIST endorsement.

Any project-specific methodology is independently developed for this simulated case study and is not presented as a NIST requirement.

## Project status

Portfolio prototype tested in a synthetic QA environment.

No production organization, client data or real vendor data was used.
